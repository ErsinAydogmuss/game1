# Mağaza metinleri: Google Play ve App Store

Olta'nın iki mağazadaki yazıları, Türkçe ve İngilizce. Kazı'nın `docs/play-store/magaza-metinleri.md`'si örnek alındı.
Sınırlar:

| Alan | Google Play | App Store |
|---|---|---|
| Ad | 30 karakter | 30 karakter |
| Kısa açıklama / alt başlık | 80 | 30 (Subtitle) |
| Tam açıklama | 4.000 | 4.000 (Description) |
| Anahtar kelimeler | yok | 100 (Keywords, virgülle) |
| Tanıtım metni | yok | 170 (Promotional Text; sürüm göndermeden değişir) |

Oyunda reklam var: Play "Reklam içerir" etiketini **Uygulama içeriği → Reklamlar** beyanından kendisi koyar. İki
mağazada da iletişim bilgilerinde web sitesi `https://ersinaydogmuss.github.io` (app-ads.txt orada), gizlilik politikası
`https://ersinaydogmuss.github.io/olta-gizlilik/`.

İngilizcesi: Play'de **Ana mağaza girişi → Çevirileri yönet → Kendi çevirilerini ekle → English (United States) –
en-US**; App Store'da sağ üstteki dil menüsünden **English (U.S.)** eklenir. Ekran görüntülerinin İngilizcesi de var:
`ekran/en/` ([`yayin.md`](yayin.md), "Ekran görüntüleri").

### Full description

```
Grab your rod, sit at the end of the dock and watch the lake! Shadows drift under the water: is that a little rudd, or a giant catfish? Cast in front of the shadow, wait while the float bobs, strike at just the right moment and reel it in!

Welcome to Dede's lake. Dede sits on the dock and teaches you to fish. At Nana's shop new rods, reels and baits are waiting. The lake is big, and its secrets are even bigger...

🎣 SEE THE SHADOW, CAST, STRIKE, REEL
• Watch the fish shadows swim; cast in front of the right one.
• Be patient while the float bobs, strike when it sinks!
• Keep the line tight but not too tight; big fish need good gear.
• Easy reeling mode makes it relaxing for everyone.

🐟 19 SPECIES, SIX RARITIES
• From rudd to pike, from mirror carp to a giant catfish: 19 species.
• Common, Fine, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic fish; the odds are shown in your book.
• Pity: if a rare fish takes too long, the next one is guaranteed.
• Every fish goes into your Fish Book: your biggest, how many you caught, its favorite bait.

🗺️ A BIG LAKE
• Six areas: Dock Cove, Reed Bed, Lily Cove, Rocky Point, Stream Mouth and Deep Water.
• Travel fast with the map; row a boat out to the middle of the lake.
• Float, spinning, bottom and fly fishing: each technique has its own gear and its own fish.
• Set crayfish traps that fill while you fish.

🌙 A LIVING LAKE
• Morning, noon, evening and night; clear, cloudy, rainy and foggy weather. Some fish only come out at night.
• Have tea with Dede to pass the time.
• Release fish and clean up litter: as the lake's love grows, it blooms and rare fish come more often.
• Daily requests from Dede, with rewards.

📜 THE SECRET OF THE LAKE
• Find letters in bottles floating on the water and uncover an old story.
• Collect the four pieces of the map; a foggy night waits where the X is.
• Are you ready to meet the Lake Keeper?

🏡 HOME
• The biggest of each kind swims in the aquarium on Nana's counter; visitors leave donations.
• Tekir the cat follows you around, purrs, asks for a fish when hungry and brings you gifts.
• Choose your angler; earn hats, vests and rod skins.
• Frame the lake's most beautiful moment in photo mode.

🎮 PLAY YOUR WAY
• No internet needed, play anywhere.
• Free to play: watching rewarded ads is always your choice; luck is never sold for money.
• English and Turkish.

Cast your line and uncover the secret of the lake. The Lake Keeper is waiting!
```
